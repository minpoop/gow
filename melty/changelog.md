Twenty-sixth alpha, from your play-test of 0.1.24 ("hud art is transparent", "boss is good").

- **Ragnarök's HUD art is solid now.** The bar shapes read from your install were drawn as thin, see-through strips: Ragnarök's pictures have a lot of empty space around the shape and a faint alpha. The HUD now crops each shape to what is actually drawn, makes it fully solid, and gives the bars a bronze edge with a dark body, so the health, Spartan Rage and enemy bars read clearly over any background.
- **Enemies stay tough after dying or resting.** Elden Ring puts an enemy back to its normal health when it respawns (after a rest at a Site of Grace or a reload). The mod now notices this and gives it the tripled health again, so a cleared area is as hard the second time.

Unchanged: summons (20,000 health, 3.5x damage) and Spartan Rage. Still an alpha: Elden Ring's own animations play for every move, and the runic rings keep the mod's drawing for now.
