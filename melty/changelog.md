Fourteenth alpha: Rage fixes, from your play-test of 0.1.12 (the new HUD, effect rings and stand-in sounds all passed).

- **Rage no longer fills from nothing.** Detonating spears into empty air used to earn Rage for every spear, so spamming throw + detonate filled the meter in under two minutes without touching an enemy. Spears now earn Rage only for what they hit.
- **Ordinary hits now count.** Elden Ring does not reliably record who hit an enemy, so normal swings were invisible to the mod and Rage (and the stun meter) only moved when the mod itself dealt damage, such as on kills. A hit on a nearby enemy within a moment of your own attack now counts as yours, so every swing builds Rage and stun.
- The Rage meter is back to 100 points (it was cut to 60 in 0.1.12, which is why it filled so fast).

Still an alpha: the HUD is the mod's own drawing, runics and finishers have no animation of their own, and nothing from Ragnarök's own art or sound is in use yet.
