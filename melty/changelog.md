Ninety-ninth alpha: Kratos walks, runs and sprints like he does in Ragnarök, and stands in his own idle (his hands closed into fists), Elden Ring's pose found and checked while you move, the bear fur deep black instead of silvery grey, Ragnarök's signed textures read correctly, Ragnarök's shading toward the fur's roots, the cloak hanging from a row of bones down his back, a key to switch the cloak's swing off and on while you play, the wind blowing it only back and forth behind him, a crash after a fall fixed, and the cloak hangs close around his shoulders and sides and moves more freely, without bouncing up or flying around when he turns, and its fur is drawn with Elden Ring's own hair material. Plus sharper bump detail and depth on his armour and skin, Kratos the right way round, gold stitching, his arms armoured like the rest of the Black Bear Set, his armour's textures at full size, the Blades on Backhand Blade moves, and a close-up camera.

- **Empty hands (testing aid).** A new "debug_empty_hands" setting: "except_axe" (on for now) leaves the Blades of Chaos, the Draupnir Spear and the Guardian Shield undrawn (they still work), "all" hides the axe too, "off" shows everything. The long rods at his left hand show even in the rest pose, so they look like a weapon model rather than his fingers: this tells which.
- **His back and shoulders follow his own spine.** In 0.1.96 his idle hunched his shoulders up around his head and his walk bent his back at one bone: the mod had put Elden Ring's spine, neck and left collarbone on small helper bones on Kratos's chest that move with his arms and breathing. Each now follows Kratos's own spine, neck or collarbone joint, so his back bends along its length and his shoulders sit level. The model rebuilds once on the first launch. With "the game's arms" (F8) the arms and shoulder fur still follow Elden Ring's arms while his chest follows his own, so the shoulders can pull away from his chest in that view.
- **His walk, run, sprint and idle in one piece.** In 0.1.94 none of his moves showed, and in 0.1.95 they showed with him crumpled over: the mod had misjudged how many bones Elden Ring keeps in his pose (150, then 167, where there are 181). It now works the count out from how far apart the game keeps its two copies of his pose.
- **Kratos walks, runs and sprints his own way.** Along with his idle, the mod now reads his Ragnarök walk, run and sprint from your install and plays them on him. When you move forward at a walk, a run or a sprint, he uses the one nearest your speed, played at your actual speed so his feet keep pace with the ground, and he blends from one to the next without breaking stride. Anything else (rolling, attacking, jumping, backing up while locked on) stays Elden Ring's own animation, and the game hands straight back to it the moment you do one. The model rebuilds once on the first launch to add them. With "debug_tools" on, F8 cycles: his moves; his moves with the game's arms (the weapon stays in his hands); Elden Ring's rest pose (a check); the game's own animation. His axe still floats beside him while his own moves play.
- **His hands fixed in the idle.** In 0.1.91 and 0.1.92 his hands came out stretched flat against his legs. Elden Ring's fingers have three bones each, Kratos's four or five joints, and the mod had paired them one by one by position: a finger's middle bone could follow his fingertip (the little finger folded backwards) and the thumb didn't move at all, so it stuck straight out of the fist. Now each finger is matched to one of his whole finger chains, and his idle closes both hands into proper fists (checked outside the game on your own model, which you sent). The model rebuilds once on the first launch. F8 still cycles the idle, the idle with the game's arms (the axe stays in his hands), Elden Ring's rest pose, and the game's pose. The axe still floats beside him in his own idle: that's next. Also, the idle is now only written into the game's two live copies of his pose, not three others it never or rarely updates.
- **Kratos's own idle.** When you stand still, Kratos now stands the way he does in Ragnarök: his combat stance, weight shifting and breathing, instead of Elden Ring's. The mod reads his idle from your Ragnarök install when it builds his model (the first launch of this version rebuilds it once), fits it to your character's skeleton, and plays it over the game's pose. As soon as you walk, roll, attack or do anything else, it hands back to Elden Ring's own animation in about a tenth of a second, and takes over again after you've stood still for half a second. The cloak still swings on top of it. Things to watch for: his weapons are placed by the game, so they may not sit in his hands while the idle plays (the left one especially); that's next on the list.
- **Where Elden Ring keeps his pose: found.** The mod now knows where the game keeps Kratos's pose each frame and checked it while you move, which is what lets his own moves play on top of it.
- **Groundwork for Kratos's own movement.** Ragnarök's animation format is worked out, so the mod can read his moves from your install.
- **Black fur, not silver.** The bear cloak's fur is nearly black in Ragnarök, but in Elden Ring it caught a grey-blue sheen, on either fur material. Elden Ring makes every surface that isn't metal reflect a little light whatever its colour, and over thousands of fur strands facing the sky that added up to a silvery coat. The fur is now treated as mostly metal, which reflects in its own colour: its black roots reflect next to nothing, and its lighter tips still catch the light. The fur around the shoulder pad gets the same. The first start after updating rebuilds his body.
- **Bump maps read the right way.** Ragnarök stores some textures, bump maps among them, as signed values, and the mod read them as unsigned. A bump map read that way tilts its flat parts all the way over, so the light hit them from the wrong angles. The fur around his shoulder pad has one of these, which is likely part of why the light on the fur looked off. Other textures stored the same way, if any, are fixed too. The first start after updating rebuilds his body.
- **Ragnarök's shading on the fur.** The fur around his shoulder pad now carries Ragnarök's occlusion map (shading that darkens it toward the roots and under the strands above), baked into its colour because Elden Ring's materials have no place for it. The bear cloak's own fur has no such map or bump map in Ragnarök's files, so it is as before.
- **The cloak's bottom stays behind him.** Once he had moved, the bottom edge of the cloak went off to his left and stayed there, even with the cloak's swing switched off. So the swing wasn't the cause: the bones the cloak's lower half hung from move by themselves in Elden Ring, most likely with his legs (his two-handed stance stands with his legs apart). The cloak now hangs from a single row of bones down the middle of his back, the ones Elden Ring hangs its own capes from, which follow only his back and hips. Its two sides now swing together rather than each on its own. The first start after updating rebuilds his body.
- **Cloak swing off and on (testing aid).** While "debug_tools" is on, F7 switches the cloak's swing off and on as you play. Off, the cloak hangs exactly as it was built and only his back and hips carry it, so you can see whether something odd about it comes from the swing or from its shape. Change the key with "debug_cape_key" in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\GowRing\settings.json`.
- **The wind only behind him.** Once he had moved, the cloak could drift to one side of his back and stay there. The mod's wind blows from one direction in the world, so whenever he faced side-on to it, it held the cloak against the furthest it may go to that side (about 12 cm at the hem) for as long as he kept facing that way. The wind now only blows the cloak back and forth behind him. It still swings to the sides when he turns or steps sideways, and then comes back.
- **A crash after falling.** About 7 seconds after a fall hurt him, the game crashed in the mod: the game was rebuilding the player, and the mod read his model before it was there. The mod now waits until it's back.
- **A calmer cloak that still moves.** In 0.1.78 the cloak bounced up whenever he started walking and flew around when he turned on the spot: the mod moved it as if it hung in the air around him, so each step yanked it like a pendulum and a quick turn swung it right round. It now moves and turns with him, trailing behind when he walks and more when he runs, swaying when he speeds up, slows down or turns, and blowing in the wind (0.1.79 was a little stiff; it's looser now). The cloak also hangs close around his shoulders and sides now. Elden Ring's rest pose holds the arms raised, and the cloak's sides lay over them, so with his arms down they stood out from him (an air pocket at the shoulder, a box from behind), and in 0.1.80 they stretched when he moved an arm. The mod now shapes the cloak for his arms down and hangs it from his shoulders, so his arms move under it. The first start after updating rebuilds his body.
- **Fur on Elden Ring's hair material.** The bear cloak's fur, and the tufts around his shoulder pad, are made of strands on cards. Until now they were drawn with the material Torrent's mane uses, which cuts each strand out sharply and lights it like armour. They now use the material Elden Ring gives its characters' hair, which should make the strands softer and catch the light more like fur. If you prefer the old look, set "hair_fur" to false in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\GowRing\settings.json` to go back. The first start after updating rebuilds his body.
- **Fur you can see.** The bear cloak's fur is black in Ragnarök already, with lighter tips on its strands. The mod had been dyeing it black on top, which flattened it to one even black and hid the strands. It now keeps Ragnarök's own colour, so the strands and their tips show.
- **Sharper bump detail.** His bump maps (the depth in leather, metal, stitching and muscle) are now used at their full size, up to 2048 pixels, instead of half. The first start after updating rebuilds his body.
- **Depth on his armour.** The gauntlet the shield comes out of looked like a flat picture, and so did his chest harness, belt, apron, arm guards, torso and trousers. Their bump maps (the textures that give leather, metal and muscle their depth) were never used, because Ragnarök cuts long texture names short and the mod couldn't tell from the name which texture was the bump map. It now tells by the texture itself. The first start after updating rebuilds his body.
- **The right way round.** Kratos had been mirrored left to right ever since the mod first built him: his tattoo ran over his right eye instead of his left, and his armour sat on the wrong sides (it showed most on his arms, where the wraps and the bracer were swapped). Ragnarök and Elden Ring put a character's left on opposite sides, and the mod hadn't turned him over. He's now as in Ragnarök. His weapons aren't affected.
- **Gold stitching.** The zigzag stitching on his harness, shoulder pad and belt is now amber gold, as in the set's art, instead of a red orange.
- **Armoured arms.** Kratos now wears the Black Bear Set's own arm guards over his upper arms and its own bracers, in black with their metal kept, instead of bare upper arms and another outfit's bracers. They come from your Ragnarök install like the rest of the set. The first start after updating rebuilds his body.
- **Sharper armour.** The mod had been halving Ragnarök's larger textures. His armour now uses them at their full size (up to 2048 pixels), so the leather, metal and stitching show their full detail. His body file is bigger, so the first start after updating takes a little longer.
- **The cloak moves.** The back of the bear cloak now swings from his shoulder blades down: it trails behind him as he walks and runs, swings when he turns, sags a little standing still, and blows in a gusting wind. In 0.1.71 only the part below his waist moved, and the part hanging by his right arm stayed with the arm; now that part goes with the rest of the cloak, and the upper back moves too. In 0.1.72 it went through him when the wind blew from behind or he stopped running; now it never swings in front of where it hangs, keeps more room behind his legs the faster he goes, and only goes so far to either side. (In 0.1.70 it didn't move at all for anyone who had played 0.1.68 or 0.1.69: their settings file had it switched off. It's switched on once.) Elden Ring's own capes use cloth data the mod can't make, so the mod moves the cloak itself: it hangs from bones under his spine and hips, so it follows his back in rolls and attacks. It doesn't yet react to his own spine's movement (only his running, turning and the wind), and the mod can't see where his arms and legs are, so an arm may still go through it at times. Set "cape_wind" in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\GowRing\settings.json` (0 to 3) for more or less wind, or "cape_physics" to false to keep it still (both at the next Play).
- **No bare skin on his back by the shoulder pad.** The bear cloak's fur is made of see-through strands, and under it Ragnarök puts a fur-covered hide over his upper back and left shoulder. The mod had been treating that hide as strands too, found no strands in it and left it out, so his skin showed through the cloak by the shoulder pad. It's drawn now, in black. The shoulder pad also gets its second panel, the laced leather band over the back of the shoulder, which was left out as if it came from another outfit. The first start after updating rebuilds his body.

0.1.58 passed its test on the new Elden Ring update: Kratos, his axe, spear and shield all showed, and it ran the whole ten minutes.

- **Blades of Chaos.** The Blades are now Kratos's own, built from your Ragnarök install like the other weapons: the first look of the blade with its first handle and pommel, one in each hand. If you have Shadow of the Erdtree, they now swing with the Backhand Blade's moves: it's a paired weapon, so with Kratos two-handing he fights with a blade in each hand. Without the DLC they keep the whip's moves, which reach less far than the whip did. A character made with an earlier version gets the change too. Their own moves come with the animation work. While you hold L1 the left blade goes on his back and the shield comes out.
- **Two-handed by default, shield on L1.** Kratos holds his weapon in both hands, with the Guardian Shield put away. Hold L1 (guard) and he takes the weapon in one hand and the shield comes out to block or parry; let go and it's put away again. It doesn't fold out of his bracer yet (that's animation work): it just appears. It works by switching his left hand to an empty weapon slot, so keep one left slot empty. To go back to Elden Ring's own stance and two-hand toggle, set "two_hand_by_default" to false in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\GowRing\settings.json`, or set "hide_stowed_shield" to false to have the shield hang on his back (both apply within a second or two).
- **Black Bear Set.** Kratos wears the Black Bear Set from Ragnarök's New Game+ update: the heavy bear-fur cloak from the start of the game, a leather harness across his chest with its shoulder pad, a broad belt with silver plates and a knot buckle, and a riveted leather apron over a torn hide, all in black with orange lacing (the silver and rivets stay as they are), plus black leggings and boots. Until now the mod built the set on the wrong waist: another outfit's narrow belt and a red cloth with a gold pattern hanging in front, which left his belly bare from the belt up and looked like a beer gut. The cloak's fur is made of strands on cards, and 0.1.61 and 0.1.62 drew the cards as black and white blocks; they're now cut to the strands with the cloak's own fur texture. The black is a dye on Ragnarök's own textures, so it may not match the game exactly yet. To go back to the Survival Set with the leather cloak, set "black_bear_outfit" to false in the same file; it applies the next time you press Play.
- **Less shiny.** Kratos's skin, leather and fur looked wet. Elden Ring reads how shiny a surface is from one colour channel of its texture, and the mod had been writing it the wrong way round: the dullest parts came out the shiniest, and every earlier attempt to tone it down made it worse. It's now the right way round, and parts with no shine of their own (like the fur) are matte. The bear fur is also shaded as one soft surface instead of hundreds of separate strips catching the light.
- **Close-up camera.** Press F11 on the keyboard to bring the camera in close to Kratos's chest, press again to come nearer, again for his waist, and once more to go back to the normal camera. Turn around him with the camera stick or mouse as usual. It's a testing aid for looking at the model: it's listed on the controls panel, and it goes with the other testing keys (set "debug_tools" to false to turn them off, or pick another key with "debug_camera_key").
- **Nothing goes missing up close.** 0.1.63 drew the chest strap in the character creator and lost it in the world: the game skips a piece of a model when the box around its resting place is off screen, and the strap's was small. Every piece now carries the whole body's box.
- **Elden Ring 2.7.2 (patch 1.17.2).** Steam updated Elden Ring, and the mod now needs this version: it finds the game's data through a table of addresses that changes with every patch. Older versions of the game are no longer supported. 0.1.57 crashed on entering the world because the patch also moved the game's list of nearby characters; this version reads it where it now is.

- **Leviathan Axe.** Ragnarök keeps all five looks of the axe in one model, each wrapped in a frost shell, plus 27 pommel knobs and seven colours of each runic gem. 0.1.55 drew nearly all of them at once, which made it look frozen. Now it's the axe Kratos starts with: its first look, the plain leather-wrapped grip end and a blue gem in each socket, with no ice.
- **Guardian Shield.** The shield model holds every shield type. 0.1.55 kept half of the Guardian Shield's plates and rim and mixed in pieces of the others. Now it's the whole Guardian Shield: hub, spine and clasps, rim, and all 32 plates. It was also on back to front (you saw its inside): it now faces out, and sits in front of the hand instead of around it.
- **Draupnir Spear.** One look instead of three, with its own butt instead of a stand-in wood, and no flag. Elden Ring has no cloth physics for it, so it would hang stiff.
- **Hands on the handles.** The hand now closes on a plain part of each handle. The axe's handle is slimmed to an Elden Ring grip where the hand covers it, so the fingers no longer sink into it.
- **Shield bash.** The shield has the hit point Elden Ring's shield attacks use.
- **Gjallarhorn is back.** Heimdall's horn hangs on the pelt over the axe holster again. The spot there that looked wrong was the fur, not the horn.
- **Fur on the cloak.** The fur by the claw on the upper back showed as grey blocks: it is made of strands on cards, and is now drawn see-through where the strands aren't, like the beard.
- The first start after updating rebuilds Kratos's body and weapons.
