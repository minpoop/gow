Twenty-ninth alpha, from your notes on the Ragnarök HUD ("the fire around L3 and R3 should be animated", "the runic attacks should be able to change", "not all runic attacks are blue"). It also carries 0.1.25 to 0.1.27, which were never played.

- **Choose your runic attacks.** Each weapon now has three light and three heavy runics, each with its own range, shape and element. Hold **L1** and press **D-pad up** (light) or **D-pad down** (heavy) to put the next one in that slot. A panel shows the weapon's runics with the chosen one lit and what it does, and the plate on the HUD changes to its symbol. Your choice is saved in `settings.json`, where you can also type a runic's name (the file lists the choices for every slot).
  - Leviathan Axe, light: Frost Shockwave, Rime Ring, Ice Lance. Heavy: Glacial Cleave, Winter's Maw, Frostfall.
  - Blades of Chaos, light: Flame Lash, Ember Whip, Cinder Rush. Heavy: Inferno Whirl, Pyre Pillar, Scorched Arc.
  - Draupnir Spear, light: Spark Volley, Shard Burst, Thunder Step. Heavy: Spear Storm, Blast Ring, Sky Lance.
- **Runic plates take the weapon's colour.** Frozen blue for the Axe, glowing red-orange for the Blades, sandy yellow for the Spear. Swapping weapons changes the plates, their symbols and the weapon icon together.
- **The L3 + R3 fire is alive.** The flames around the buttons lick, sway and flicker, and embers drift up off them while Spartan Rage is ready.
- **The HUD looks like Ragnarök's** (0.1.26 to 0.1.27). It sits in the bottom-left corner, placed and coloured from your Ragnarök screenshot:
  - **Shield:** on a green octagon.
  - **Runic attacks:** diamond plates that go dark and refill from the bottom up after use.
  - **Weapon:** a white icon on a grey disc.
  - **Health:** a green bar glowing toward its end.
  - **Spartan Rage:** four red segments; the one still filling burns orange.
- **Enemies stay tough after dying or resting** (0.1.25). Respawned enemies get their tripled health back.
- The BURNING / FROZEN labels over enemies no longer carry a stray "MIMIR" tag.

Unchanged: summons (20,000 health, 3.5x damage) and Spartan Rage. Still an alpha: Elden Ring's own animations play for every move, and runics have no cast animation yet (their effect, ring and sound play).
