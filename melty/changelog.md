Thirty-ninth alpha. From your 0.1.37 test ("the green stones work as intended. the roar is now there") and your next goal, "i now want to push to get the actually animations".

- **The Rage roar no longer gets lost when you're hit.** In your log the first roar didn't play because Kratos was still staggered when you set Rage off. The mod now keeps trying for up to two seconds, so the roar plays as soon as he can move.
- **Kratos's model, for putting the real Kratos in Elden Ring.** From your idea to get the model first: the background check now also saves Kratos's own model data from your Ragnarök install (his mesh, its vertex data and the materials, about 10 MB) to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\GowRing\kratos_probe\model`, so the format can be worked out. The plan is to fit his body onto Elden Ring's skeleton, so every move in the game animates him; his own Ragnarök animations can then follow on the same skeleton.
- **A bigger look at Kratos's animations, for bringing his real moves over.** About three minutes into play, the background check now reads through Kratos's animation files from your Ragnarök install. It lists every animation in them and saves a few whole ones (idle, axe, blades and spear attacks, Rage, dodges) to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\GowRing\kratos_probe` on your PC. This happens once, takes up to a few minutes in the background, and may make the game stutter briefly while it reads. Nothing from it is used in game yet, and nothing leaves your PC unless you send it.

Still an alpha: finishers, throws and recalls have no animation yet, and every move is still Elden Ring's own.
