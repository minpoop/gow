Thirty-eighth alpha, from your 0.1.36 test: "there was no animation for the start of rage i just crouch and i did not see any green stones".

- **Spartan Rage starts with a roar.** Kratos now plays Elden Ring's Beast's Roar as Rage takes hold: a roar with a shockwave in front of him. It goes through the game's normal skill system, the same way the runic animations play and end, so it can't trap you like the 0.1.35 roar did. Your weapon gets its own skill back once the roar is done, and the roar costs no FP.
- **No more crouching when you press L3 + R3.** L3 on its own is Elden Ring's crouch, so if your thumb hit L3 a moment before R3, Kratos crouched. While the Rage meter is full, L3 now only starts Rage. When the meter isn't full, L3 crouches as usual, and R3 is always lock-on.
- **Health stones drop more reliably.** They only dropped when the mod could tell you landed the killing blow, and Elden Ring doesn't always say. Now any enemy that dies within 25 m of you can drop one, whoever killed it. The chance is up from 3 in 5 to 3 in 4, and a boss still drops three. Each drop is written to the log.

Still an alpha: finishers, throws and recalls have no animation yet.
