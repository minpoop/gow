Twenty-seventh alpha, from your play-test of 0.1.24 ("hud art is transparent", "boss is good") and your Ragnarök screenshot.

- **The HUD is laid out like Ragnarök's.** Everything Kratos needs now sits together in the bottom-left corner, as in Ragnarök: the shield on an octagon where Ragnarök shows the relic, the light and heavy runics stacked as a pair (light on top, heavy below, with a small arrow for D-pad up and down), the weapon on a round plate, the health bar with the Spartan Rage bar under it, and the L3 + R3 prompt under that when Rage is full. The weapon's name shows for a moment when you switch. Mimir's subtitles moved up a little so they never cover the bars. `hud_scale` in `settings.json` still makes the whole cluster bigger or smaller.
- **Ragnarök's HUD art is solid now.** The bar shapes read from your install were drawn as thin, see-through strips: Ragnarök's pictures have a lot of empty space around the shape and a faint alpha. The HUD now crops each shape to what is actually drawn, makes it fully solid, and gives the bars a bronze edge with a dark body.
- **Enemies stay tough after dying or resting.** Elden Ring puts an enemy back to its normal health when it respawns (after a rest at a Site of Grace or a reload). The mod now notices this and gives it the tripled health again.

Unchanged: summons (20,000 health, 3.5x damage) and Spartan Rage. Still an alpha: Elden Ring's own animations play for every move, and the runic rings keep the mod's drawing for now.
