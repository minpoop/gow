Fifty-second alpha: the cloak moves with Kratos's arms, the face under his head is hidden, and his full beard is back.

0.1.50 ran a full 10-minute test with no crash.

- **The cloak moves with the arms.** In Ragnarök the cloak, shoulder fur and skirt are cloth, moved by physics Elden Ring doesn't have. Until now they stayed with the torso, so the arm pushed out through the side of the cloak. Now each piece of cloth follows the body under it: the cloak and fur follow the arm, and the skirt partly follows the legs.
- **No face at the neck.** Kratos's empty helmet hid your character's head and hair, but your character's face was still drawn and showed around Kratos's neck. The helmet now shrinks that face to a sliver inside Kratos's own head, the same way Elden Ring's helmets keep faces from poking through. This works on characters that start with the helmet (new characters since 0.1.47).
- **The full beard (first try).** Kratos's long beard, eyebrows and eyelashes are made of hair strands that need see-through textures, so until now only his short under-beard showed. They now use Elden Ring's cut-out material (the one Torrent uses), with Ragnarök's own hair textures from your install. If the game can't use it, the beard just stays as it was, and the log says what was found.
- **Rebuilds Kratos once.** The first start after updating rebuilds Kratos's model from your Ragnarök files (about as long as before).
